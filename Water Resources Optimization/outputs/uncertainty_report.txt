  3. End-of-horizon storage varies significantly across scenarios, indicating
     that the optimal release policy is sensitive to the realised inflow.

  4. The boxplot of releases shows which days are most affected by inflow
     uncertainty.  Wider boxes imply greater sensitivity to forecast errors.

  5. These results highlight the value of accurate inflow forecasting for
     reservoir operation.  A robust (stochastic) optimisation approach could
     reduce downside risk by explicitly considering the distribution of inflows.
